Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Gaming Clip Uploader is a personal, single-user desktop application. It uses Google OAuth solely to upload videos and read scheduling information on the account owner's own YouTube channel via the YouTube Data API.

  • The application runs entirely on the owner's personal computer.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on that computer and are never transmitted to any third party or external server.
  • The application does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data from any user.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind is used.
  • Google user data accessed via the API (video and schedule metadata) is used only at the moment of upload/scheduling and is not retained beyond local result logs on the owner's machine.

Access granted to this application can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
